# Universal Multilingual Human Writing Guide

This guide establishes the rules and principles for generating authentic, human-sounding articles across **any of the 100+ languages** supported by Google Translate and modern large language models.

---

## 1. The Core Universal Engine

AI text sounds robotic regardless of whether it is written in English, Spanish, French, Japanese, Hindi, Indonesian, or Arabic. Machine models fall into identical stylistic traps across all languages:

| Universal Trap | What AI Does Across All Languages | How This Skill Fixes It |
|---|---|---|
| **Formulaic Intros** | Throat-clearing clichés ("In today's fast-paced world...", "En el mundo dinámico de hoy...", "De nos jours...", "आज की तेजी से बदलती दुनिया में...") | Start directly with a practical observation and the real problem in 1–2 sentences. |
| **Rigid Cadence** | Sentences are all medium-long (18–25 words) with uniform clause structure | Enforce rhythm variety: at least 25% short sentences (3–8 words), average under 17 words. |
| **Corporate Buzzwords** | Relies on high-frequency filler ("crucial", "tapestry", "seamless", "optimizar", "essentiel", "গেম চেঞ্জার") | Strip corporate words via language-specific banned phrase files. |
| **Artificial Wrap-Ups** | Closes with summary headings ("Conclusion", "Final Thoughts", "En conclusion", "Fazit", "Kesimpulan") | Drop conclusion sections completely. The article stops naturally on the last practical step. |
| **Fake Authority** | Fabricates test results, timings, or benchmarks when none exist | Strict honesty framework: when no user notes exist, write general opinions only. |

## 2. Global Language Family Strategies

### Romance Languages (Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, Romanian)
- **Voice**: Conversational peer-to-peer tone. Use standard informal address (`tú` in Spanish, `você`/`tu` in Portuguese, `tu` in French/Italian) unless a formal technical audience is explicitly requested.
- **Rhythm**: Romance languages naturally use more connecting prepositions. Actively break long subordinate clauses into separate declarative sentences.
- **Avoid**: Formal academic essay markers (*"en conclusión"*, *"cabe destacar"*, *"il convient de noter"*, *"é mister ressaltar"*, *"este demn de remarcat"*).

### Germanic Languages (German, Dutch, Scandinavian, English)
- **Voice**: Pragmatic, direct, action-oriented.
- **Syntax**: Avoid heavy nesting (*Schachtelsätze*) in German and Dutch. Prefer active verbs over nominalized nouns (*Substantivierung*). In Scandinavian languages (Swedish, Danish, Norwegian), maintain punchy, grounded technical phrasing.
- **Avoid**: Stiff administrative jargon (*"zusammenfassend lässt sich sagen"*, *"von entscheidender Bedeutung"*, *"det råder ingen tvekan om"*).

### East Asian & CJK Languages (Japanese, Chinese, Korean)
- **Voice**: Polite yet pragmatic professional tone. In Japanese, use natural *desu/masu* without polite inflation (*keigo* overload).
- **Rhythm**: Avoid machine-translation artifacts (*hon'yaku-chou* in Japanese, translationese in Chinese). Write how native practitioners explain problems on tech forums (Qiita, Zenn, Zhihu).
- **Avoid**: Westernized conclusion templates (*"総括として"*, *"总之"*, *"결론적으로"*) and buzzwords (*"画期的な"*, *"革命性"*).

### South Asian & Brahmic Languages (Hindi, Bengali, Urdu)
- **Voice**: Conversational, grounded technical peer voice. Blend natural loan words (tech terms like cache, container, terminal) naturally rather than forcing hyper-formal Sanskritized or Persianized vocabulary.
- **Syntax**: Use standard natural word order (SOV). Avoid machine-like declarative endings repeated every sentence.
- **Avoid**: Essay clichés (*"निष्कर्ष के रूप में"*, *"উপসংহার স্বরূপ"*, *"حتمی نتیجہ"*).

### Southeast Asian & Austronesian Languages (Indonesian, Malay, Vietnamese, Tagalog/Filipino, Thai)
- **Voice**: Direct, approachable, community-oriented.
- **Syntax**: Indonesian and Malay benefit from clean SVO sentences without excessive passives (`di-` prefix overuse). Vietnamese should use clear, crisp declarative phrases rather than bureaucratic parallel structures.
- **Avoid**: Promotional translationese (*"mengubah lanskap digital"*, *"khai phá toàn bộ tiềm năng"*).

### Slavic Languages (Russian, Polish, Ukrainian, Czech)
- **Voice**: Direct, realistic, no-nonsense technical peer voice.
- **Syntax**: Keep word order natural; avoid calques translated directly from English sentence structures.

### Middle Eastern & RTL Languages (Arabic, Persian/Farsi, Hebrew)
- **Voice**: Pragmatic, clear technical phrasing.
- **Syntax**: Natural right-to-left flow without repetitive ornate rhetorical flourishes.
- **Avoid**: Flowery rhetorical transitions (*"لا ريب فيه"*, *"بی‌شک و تردید"*).

### African Languages (Swahili)
- **Voice**: Conversational, practical community peer tone.
- **Syntax**: Natural Bantu noun class concord and punchy verbs.

## 3. Side-by-Side Multilingual Showcase

To see how the human voice adapts across global languages without sounding like machine translation:

### 🇺🇸 English
> **Robotic AI**: *"In today's fast-paced DevOps environment, optimizing container build performance is crucial for seamless developer workflows. In this section, let us explore..."*
>
> **Human Writer**: *"Waiting six minutes for a one-line code change to build inside a container gets painful fast. Once you see where the cache breaks, you can usually drop that down under thirty seconds."*

### 🇪🇸 Spanish
> **Robotic AI**: *"En el vertiginoso mundo tecnológico actual, es crucial optimizar las imágenes de contenedores para garantizar una experiencia fluida. Sin duda, en conclusión..."*
>
> **Human Writer**: *"Esperar seis minutos por un cambio de una sola línea dentro de un contenedor resulta agotador. Cuando descubres dónde se invalida la memoria caché, normalmente lo reduces a treinta segundos."*

### 🇫🇷 French
> **Robotic AI**: *"Dans le paysage technologique moderne, il est essentiel d'optimiser le pipeline de conteneurs afin de débloquer le plein potentiel de vos équipes. En somme..."*
>
> **Human Writer**: *"Attendre six minutes pour compiler une ligne de code dans un conteneur devient vite insupportable. Dès qu'on repère où le cache saute, on règle ça en trente secondes."*

### 🇩🇪 German
> **Robotic AI**: *"In der heutigen dynamischen Welt ist es von entscheidender Bedeutung, Container-Builds nahtlos zu optimieren. Zusammenfassend lässt sich sagen..."*
>
> **Human Writer**: *"Sechs Minuten auf einen Build wegen einer einzigen Codezeile zu warten, nervt gewaltig. Wenn man erst weiß, wo der Cache abbricht, dauert das Ganze keine dreißig Sekunden."*

### 🇮🇩 Indonesian
> **Robotic AI**: *"Dalam lanskap digital yang terus berkembang saat ini, sangat penting untuk mengoptimalkan build container demi efisiensi yang mulus. Tanpa basa-basi lagi..."*
>
> **Human Writer**: *"Menunggu enam menit hanya untuk build ulang satu baris kode di container bikin jenuh. Begitu kita tahu di mana cache-nya rusak, biasanya waktu build bisa turun sampai di bawah tiga puluh detik."*

### 🇯🇵 Japanese
> **Robotic AI**: *"今日の急速に変化する開発環境において、コンテナビルドを最適化することはシームレスなワークフローに極めて重要です。結論として..."*
>
> **Human Writer**: *"たった1行のコード修正でコンテナのビルドに6分も待たされるのは耐えられません。どこでキャッシュが無効化されているかを特定できれば、大抵は30秒以内に短縮できます。"*

### Tier 2: Universal Extensible Architecture (100+ Additional Languages)

If your target language is not in the curated Tier 1 list above (e.g. Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Estonian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Slovak, Slovenian, etc.):
1. **Pass the ISO code**: `python scripts/check_article.py article.md --lang <code>`
2. **Universal linting executes automatically**: The checker evaluates word count target tolerances, section length symmetry variance, sentence cadence, heading depth, list-of-three constructs, code block fence isolation, and claimed experience honesty rules.
3. **Drop-in custom phrase packs**: Add native buzzwords and clichés by creating `references/banned-phrases-<code>.txt` with `[hard]` and `[soft]` blocks. The linter auto-discovers and enforces them immediately.
